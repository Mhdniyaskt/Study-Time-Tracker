# Study Time Tracker - History Feature User Guide

## 🎯 Quick Start

### Access History
1. Open your Study Time Tracker dashboard
2. Click the **"History"** button in the top-right corner
3. You'll see all your study sessions by default (All Time view)

---

## 📅 Using Period Filters

### Quick Filters (One Click)

**Today**
- Shows sessions from today only
- Great for checking daily progress

**Yesterday**
- Shows sessions from yesterday
- Compare yesterday vs today

**This Week**
- Shows Monday to today
- See weekly progress

**Last Week**
- Shows previous Monday-Sunday
- Compare to current week

**This Month**
- Shows 1st of month to today
- Track monthly progress

**Last Month**
- Shows entire previous month
- Monthly review and analysis

**All Time**
- Shows every session ever
- Complete study history

**Custom Range**
- Click to open date picker
- Select start date
- Select end date
- Click "Apply Date Range"
- View any date range you want

---

## 📊 Understanding Statistics

### Total Study Time
Shows sum of all sessions in selected period.
- **Example:** "10h 55m" means 10 hours and 55 minutes total

### Sessions
Shows count of study sessions.
- **Example:** "6 sessions" means 6 separate study sessions

### Average per Session
Shows average duration per session.
- **Example:** "1h 49m" means average session is 1 hour 49 minutes
- **Calculation:** Total time ÷ Number of sessions

### Study by Subject
Shows breakdown by subject with visual bars.
- Subjects sorted by most time first
- **Example:** "Mathematics: 2h 30m"
- Bar width shows proportion of total time

### Daily Totals
Shows how much you studied each day.
- Newest dates first
- **Example:** "Thu, Sep 3: 6h 55m"
- Scroll to see all days

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Data Safety
- **All your data is safe** - nothing gets deleted when you change filters
- Filters only change what you *see*, not what's stored
- Your complete history is always preserved

### Edit/Delete from History
- You can edit sessions directly from history page
- Delete requires confirmation (are you sure?)
- After edit/delete, you stay on history page

### Date Format
- Dates shown as "Thu, Sep 3" for readability
- Full date shown in session groups
- Custom range uses YYYY-MM-DD format

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I see sessions from 6 months ago?**
A: Yes! Use "All Time" or custom date range.

**Q: Does filtering delete old data?**
A: No! Filtering only changes the view. All data is permanent.

**Q: Can I export my history?**
A: Not yet, but this could be a future feature.

**Q: Why don't I see any sessions?**
A: Either no sessions exist for that period, or check the date range.

**Q: How far back does history go?**
A: Forever! Every session you've ever added is stored.

**Q: Can I compare two custom periods side-by-side?**
A: Currently, you view one period at a time. Future enhancement idea!

**Q: What happens if I delete a session from history?**
A: It's permanently deleted from all periods (confirmation required).
