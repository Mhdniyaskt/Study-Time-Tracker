# What's New: Daily Goals & Streaks Update

## Summary

This update focuses your study tracking on **daily goals** and builds a meaningful **streak system** based on achieving those goals consistently.

## What Changed

### 🎯 Daily Goal Now Defaults to 2 Hours

**Before:** Default was 3 hours per day  
**Now:** Default is 2 hours per day

- More achievable for most students
- Still fully customizable through Settings
- Your existing custom goal (if you set one) is unchanged

---

### 📊 Weekly Goal Removed

**Before:** Weekly goal of 21 hours with progress bar  
**Now:** Weekly total shown as a statistic only

**Why?**
- Simplifies the interface
- Focuses on daily consistency rather than weekly totals
- You can still see your weekly study time - just without a goal comparison

The "This Week" card now shows:
- Your total study time this week
- Clean display without a progress bar
- No goal comparison

---

### 🔥 Streak System Updated

**Before:** Any study session (even 1 minute) counted toward streak  
**Now:** You must reach your daily goal for the day to count

#### New Streak Rules

1. **Daily Goal Required**: Each day must reach or exceed your daily goal to count
2. **Consecutive Days**: Count backward from today
3. **Today Matters**: If today hasn't reached the goal, streak is 0
4. **Automatic Reset**: Missing a goal resets to 0

#### Example with 2-hour daily goal:

```
Day 1: 2h 30m ✅ → Streak = 1
Day 2: 2h 00m ✅ → Streak = 2  
Day 3: 1h 45m ❌ → Streak = 0 (under goal)
Day 4: 3h 00m ✅ → Streak = 1 (new streak)
```

**Why?**
- Encourages consistent, goal-oriented studying
- More meaningful than just "any study time"
- Motivates you to reach your daily target

## Getting Started

### Set Your Daily Goal

1. Click **Settings** (top-right corner)
2. Adjust "Daily Study Goal (hours)"
3. Click **Save Settings**

### Build Your Streak

1. Check your "Today's Study" progress bar
2. Study until you reach your daily goal
3. Keep it up each day to build your streak!
4. Track your progress on the dashboard

---

## Tips for Success

💡 **Start realistic**: Begin with a goal you can consistently achieve  
💡 **Study early**: Don't wait until late in the day  
💡 **Check progress**: Use the progress bar to see how close you are  
💡 **Use the timer**: Accurate tracking helps you reach your goal  
💡 **Celebrate wins**: Each day you reach your goal is a victory!
