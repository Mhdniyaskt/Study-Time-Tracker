# Study Streak Guide

## What is a Study Streak?

Your study streak tracks consecutive days where you've reached your daily study goal. It's designed to encourage consistent, goal-oriented studying habits.

## How It Works

### Streak Rules

1. **Daily Goal Required**: A day only counts toward your streak if you study **at least** your daily goal amount
2. **Consecutive Days**: The streak counts backward from today, checking each previous day
3. **Automatic Reset**: If any day doesn't meet the goal, your streak resets to 0
4. **Today Matters**: If today hasn't reached the goal yet, your streak is 0 (even if yesterday did)

### Examples

**Example 1: Building a Streak (2-hour daily goal)**
- Monday: Study 2h 30m → ✅ Streak = 1 day
- Tuesday: Study 2h 0m → ✅ Streak = 2 days  
- Wednesday: Study 3h 15m → ✅ Streak = 3 days
- Thursday: Study 2h 45m → ✅ Streak = 4 days

**Example 2: Streak Reset (2-hour daily goal)**
- Monday: Study 2h 30m → ✅ Streak = 1 day
- Tuesday: Study 2h 0m → ✅ Streak = 2 days
- Wednesday: Study 1h 45m → ❌ Only 1h 45m (under goal) → Streak = 0
- Thursday: Study 3h 0m → ✅ New streak starts → Streak = 1 day

**Example 3: Today Determines Streak (2-hour daily goal)**
- Yesterday: Study 3h 0m
- Today: Study 1h 30m so far
- **Current Streak: 0** (today hasn't reached the goal yet)
- Once you study another 30+ minutes today: Streak = 2 days

## Setting Your Daily Goal

### Default Goal
- The default daily goal is **2 hours** (120 minutes)

### Changing Your Goal

1. Click the **Settings** button in the top-right corner
2. Adjust "Daily Study Goal (hours)" 
3. You can set any value from 0.5 to 24 hours in 0.5-hour increments
4. Click **Save Settings**
5. Your new goal takes effect immediately

### Goal Tips

- **Too Easy?** Increase your goal to challenge yourself
- **Too Hard?** Lower your goal to something more achievable
- **Finding Balance**: Your goal should be challenging but realistic for your schedule
- **Consistency Wins**: A smaller goal you can meet daily is better than a large goal you rarely reach

## Streak Strategy

### Building Your Streak

1. **Start Small**: Begin with a comfortable goal you can consistently achieve
2. **Study Early**: Don't wait until late in the day - start early to ensure you reach your goal
3. **Track Progress**: Check your "Today's Study" card throughout the day
4. **Plan Ahead**: Know when you'll study each day to maintain consistency

### Maintaining Your Streak

- Use the timer to track study sessions accurately
- Break study time into multiple sessions if needed (they all count toward your daily total)
- All subjects count - variety is fine!
- Weekend days count the same as weekdays

### What Breaks a Streak?

❌ Missing a full day (no study sessions)  
❌ Studying but not reaching your daily goal  
❌ Changing your goal doesn't reset your streak, but future days use the new goal

### What Doesn't Break a Streak?

✅ Studying more than your goal (any amount over counts)  
✅ Multiple short sessions (as long as the daily total reaches the goal)  
✅ Different subjects (all study time counts)  
✅ Restarting the app (streak is calculated from your session history)

## Visual Indicators

### Streak Card

The Study Streak card on your dashboard shows:
- 🔥 **Orange flame icon** when you have an active streak
- 🔥 **Gray flame icon** when your streak is 0
- **Number of days** in your current streak
- **"Keep going!"** when streak is active
- **"Reach your goal today"** when streak is 0

### Today's Progress

The "Today's Study" card shows:
- Your current study time for today
- Your daily goal
- A progress bar showing how close you are to your goal

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I studied yesterday but my streak is 0. Why?**  
A: Today must also reach the goal. Streaks count consecutive days *including today*.

**Q: Do I need to study every subject to maintain my streak?**  
A: No! Any study time counts, regardless of subject. Your total time across all subjects must reach the daily goal.

**Q: What happens if I change my daily goal?**  
A: Your streak calculation immediately uses the new goal. Past days keep their original qualification, but going forward, you'll need to meet the new goal.

**Q: Can I see which days counted toward my streak?**  
A: Check the History page to see your daily totals and compare them to your goal.

**Q: Does the study timer affect my streak?**  
A: Only when you click "Submit" on the timer. The timer must be stopped and submitted to add time to your daily total.

**Q: My streak shows 0 but I studied a lot yesterday!**  
A: Check if you reached your daily goal yesterday. Also, today's study time must reach the goal for the streak to continue.
